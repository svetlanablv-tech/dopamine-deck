How this works

Press the button and you get one card. Do the small thing. Not feeling it? Draw another. There are no wrong cards.

  • With baby: works with a child in your arms or in the room.
  • Needs a moment: door closed, nap time or after bedtime.
  • Needs a thing: a mug, a snack, a pen, your phone.

Five suits of 16: Snack, Senses, Move, Reach Out and Spark. Each card takes from 30 seconds to 5 minutes.

Mom Dopamine Deck is a set of small, kind ideas. It is not medical advice or treatment. If you feel low, anxious or overwhelmed for more than a couple of weeks, please talk to your doctor or midwife. For support, try Postpartum Support International.

No account, no tracking. The only thing saved is your "Baby in my arms" switch, on this device.
